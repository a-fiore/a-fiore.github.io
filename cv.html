CV & Bio

Download CV (PDF)

My work sits between data and policy. I am interested in how labor markets work, and who they work for: why women’s paid work so often ends where unpaid care begins, and what policy can do about it.

I currently work with the World Bank’s Development Impact Group (DECDI), where I verify the computational reproducibility of research submitted to the Policy Research Working Paper Series, re-running authors’ code and checking every exhibit against the reported results.

Before that, I spent a year as a UN Fellow on Demographic Resilience at UNFPA Bosnia and Herzegovina, leading national studies on women’s economic inactivity and on the barriers people face in having the number of children they want. From 2023 to 2025, I worked with the World Bank’s DIME group on a randomized evaluation of childcare services for market women in Kampala, Uganda. I spent eighteen months there coordinating three waves of data collection with 972 women across six markets, and co-authored the resulting paper.

Earlier, I worked at Bruegel in Brussels, where I wrote on gender budgeting in the EU budget, and at the European Institute for Gender Equality in Vilnius, where I contributed to the Gender Equality Index 2022.

I hold an MSc in Economics and Political Science from the University of Milan, including an exchange year at Sciences Po in Paris, and a BA in International Studies from the University of Trento. Over the past five years I have lived and worked in France, Lithuania, Uganda, Belgium, and Bosnia and Herzegovina.
