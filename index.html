I am a development economist working at the intersection of data and policy, with a focus on gender, care, and labor markets. I currently work with the World Bank’s Development Impact Group (DECDI), where I verify the computational reproducibility of research in the Policy Research Working Paper Series.

Previously, I coordinated the field implementation of a randomized evaluation of childcare provision for market women in Kampala, Uganda, with the World Bank’s Development Impact Evaluation (DIME) group, and led national studies on women’s economic inactivity and fertility aspirations as a UN Fellow at UNFPA Bosnia and Herzegovina. I have also worked at Bruegel and at the European Institute for Gender Equality (EIGE).

I hold an MSc in Economics and Political Science from the University of Milan.
